3 · Why this should be tractable in conformal-net and subfactor language

  • The charged object is explicit. It is the lattice-VOA simple current Ψλ=Y(e(ωs,ωf),z), not a search over 128 independent currents. Its integral conformal weight hλ=1 gives trivial self-monodromy, the arithmetic precondition for a bosonic local extension. [E] (v983)
  • The finite side is closed. The order-4 class, fusion powers, even unimodular overlattice, index-4 Q-system, conditional expectations and sector arithmetic have been computed exactly. The lattice sector count collapses from 4 to 1. [E] (v125/v154/v469/v983)
  • The quasi-free boundary is explicit. Fixed-window implementers and their convergence, the crossed-product tower, and the universal two-edge Shale obstruction are proved in the attached memorandum; the measured logarithmic coefficient agrees with the analytic value at 0.18%.
  • The neutral/bilinear half already lies in a proved convergence framework. The cited lattice-fermion scaling-limit theorem supplies strong convergence, Virasoro and correlator control, and polynomial errors for the fermion-bilinear currents. [C]
  • The residual has the expected shape for existing extension technology. What remains is to place one explicit spin-field limit in the DHR/Q-system setting, prove relative locality and energy bounds, and identify the resulting holomorphic extension. This matches the interface between Longo–Rehren/Böckenhauer–Evans simple-current extension methods and the Osborne–Stottmeister lattice convergence framework. [O]

4 · What acceptance looks like

The contract asks for one normalized simple-current/spin-field intertwiner and the following four statements:

G1 — convergence on the finite-particle core with energy bounds;

G2 — locality relative to the bilinear net;

G3 — the correct braiding/monodromy relation; and

G4 — Q-system closure.

The acceptance test is quoted from SEAM.SIMPLECURRENT.GENERATOR.01:

“A solution counts iff it (a) proves G1–G4 for one explicit Ψσ, or (b) proves that no local normalized generator exists (the registered kill — then the seam route is dead and the ledger records it as exactly that).”

Thus a negative theorem is also a complete outcome. Partial finite evidence, a character match, central charge c=8 by itself, or convergence of fermion bilinears does not satisfy the acceptance test. The hypotheses and conclusion are entirely 1+1-dimensional; no bulk or 3+1-dimensional claim is part of this handoff.
