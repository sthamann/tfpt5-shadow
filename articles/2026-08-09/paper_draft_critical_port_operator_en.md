## Abstract

We study a family of finite matrices built purely from the prime-power comb $\{\log n,\ 2\Lambda(n)/\sqrt n\}$ and explicit window geometry, whose uniform contractivity on a fixed cofinal ladder is equivalent, through a machine-checked chain of identities, to Weil positivity on a dense test family — and hence to the Riemann Hypothesis. The chain of exact reductions is: (i) the Krein floor of the window form equals $1-\lambda_{\max}(E_h)$ for a Carleson Gram matrix $E_h=\sqrt{\tilde\nu}\,K_{\mathrm{CD}}\sqrt{\tilde\nu}$ of a Christoffel–Darboux kernel (a $\pm1$ diagonal gauge removes all sign structure); (ii) a Schur complement localizes the entire criticality in a *dressed port block* $D_{P,h}$ of $\sim n/10$ nodes, exactly ($ (1-\lambda_{\max}(D_{P,h}))/\tau_h = 1.00$ on all tested rungs, Haynsworth-verified); (iii) $D_{P,h}$ has *exact displacement rank two* — it is an integrable (IIKS-class) kernel with two explicit generator vectors, a property preserved by the Schur dressing; (iv) compressed onto a fixed twelve-dimensional index window, the family converges in operator norm with rate $h^{-2.8}$ to a numerically explicit limit object while carrying the wall exactly; (v) a one-dimensional Schur pivot renders the wall as a single scalar inequality $\sigma_h = [1-\tilde\nu_{m^*}K_h(y^*,y^*)] - b^\top(I-E_{\mathrm{rest}})^{-1}b \ge 0$ whose leading term is a phase-free windowed prime functional. The source of the operator has an explicit universal limit: under $r=\sqrt{n/X}$ the weighted prime measure uniformizes to Lebesgue $dr$ (Kolmogorov distance $5\cdot10^{-4}$ at $X=10^7$), the port mass is exactly $1-e^{-1/2}$, and the port symbol is the Mellin–Cauchy kernel $1/(1+2i\tau)$ up to a window taper and a shrinking arithmetic fluctuation. An injection experiment shows that the wall margin equals injected perturbation *energy* on the same exponential law as $\tau_h$: the reduction is an exact reformulation at RH strength, with no unconditional slack. We state two theorem targets — a Case-type sum rule for the diagonal (testing) half, and a $2\times2$ Riemann–Hilbert / nonlinear steepest descent program for the critical limit (well-posedness of the IIKS resolvent closure is verified numerically to $10^{-12}$) — and record the honest negative that the smooth PNT continuum model violates the floor at finite depth: the discreteness of the primes is load-bearing.

## 1. The objects

Fix the deployed window family (frame-A zones; 42 rungs $h\le 900$). From the comb lags $c = c_{\mathrm{arch}} + c_{\mathrm{atoms}}$ define the grid density $d(\theta_j)$, the two arms $d_\pm$, and the tilde measures $\tilde\mu_+,\tilde\nu_-$ with weight $4\sin^2(\theta/2)$ on $x=\cos\theta$ (folded). Let $p_0,\dots,p_h$ be the orthonormal polynomials of $\tilde\mu_+$ (Jacobi coefficients $a_k, b_k$; measured $b_k\to 1/2$, Szegő class), $K_{\mathrm{CD}}(y,y')=\sum_{k<h}p_k(y)p_k(y')$, and

$$E_h \;=\; \sqrt{\tilde\nu}\;K_{\mathrm{CD}}\;\sqrt{\tilde\nu}\quad\text{(the Carleson Gram)}.$$

**Gauge identity.** The Krein-form defect operator of the window equals $S\,E_h\,S$ with $S=\mathrm{diag}(\operatorname{sign} p_h(y_m))$: all off-diagonal sign structure is a gauge (verified $2\cdot10^{-12}$). The diagonal is the classical Carleson testing functional $T_m=\tilde\nu_m K_h(y_m,y_m)$ — mass over Christoffel — and $\|C_h\|\le 1 \iff E_h \preceq I$.

## 2. The exact reduction chain (all machine-checked)

1. **Displacement rank two.** $[J,\Delta] = b_h(e_{h-1}r^\top - re_{h-1}^\top)$ exactly ($4\cdot10^{-14}$); at the Gauss nodes $\hat\Delta = I - b_h^2\,\Phi\,CC^\top\Phi$ with a pure Cauchy matrix $C$ against the positive measure $\omega=\tilde\nu$-weighted $p_h^2$: the scalar Herglotz carrier $v=b_h m_\omega$ exists exactly.
2. **Port Schur reduction.** With the port set (lowest $\tau$-decile) and $D_{P,h}=P+X(I-R)^{-1}X^\top$: $I-E\succeq0 \iff I-R\succeq0 \wedge I-D_{P,h}\succeq0$ (Haynsworth integer-exact, controls included). Measured: bulk margin $=420$–$45000\times\tau$; $(1-\lambda_{\max}(D_{P,h}))/\tau=1.00$ on all rungs — *the port is the wall*.
3. **Integrable class, dressing-stable.** $[Y,P]$ has rank 2 exactly (CD collapse) *and* $[Y,D_{P,h}]$ has rank 2 exactly ($\sigma_3/\sigma_1\sim10^{-14}$): the dressed operator remains an IIKS kernel with explicit generators; the resolvent stays in the class (closure verified $10^{-12}$) — the $2\times2$ Riemann–Hilbert problem is well-posed.

## 3. The universal source and the arithmetic remainder

**The honest calibration (model problem).** Replacing the prime comb by its own smooth $dr$-limit *violates* the floor at finite depth ($\tau_{\mathrm{model}}=-1.26\to-0.11$, rising toward $0^-$): the smooth PNT world is outside the wall; the *discreteness* of the prime powers is load-bearing. ## 4a. Rigidity and induction anatomy (rounds 40/41)

Three further structural facts sharpen where a proof can and cannot live.

**The determinant chain.** With $\tau_h := \det(I - D_{P,h})$, the Schur identities $\sigma_h = \det(I-E)/\det(B)$ and $\det(I-E) = \det(I-R)\det(I-D_{P,h})$ are machine-exact with positive bulk factors, so $\operatorname{sign}\tau_h = \operatorname{sign}\sigma_h$ is the wall; the Jacobi variational identity $\frac{d}{ds}\log\det(I-sD_P) = -\operatorname{tr}[(I-sD_P)^{-1}D_P]$ holds on the true family — the differential identity any tau-function identification must satisfy. Measured: $\operatorname{slope}(\log\sigma) = -2.93$ against twice the Mellin-fluctuation slope $-2.53$ ($\sigma \sim \text{fluctuation}^2$ within 16%).

**Global rigidity (no smoothing parametrix).** The prime-power lattice with smooth quadrature masses violates the floor by orders of magnitude more than the plain continuum ($\tau = -78..-1706$ vs $-0.11..-1.26$), even when local $\pm\tfrac12$-log-unit mass sums are preserved exactly ($-78..-5537$); and smoothing *either* zone alone (edge $u>U-1$, or interior) kills the floor on every rung. The exact $\Lambda$-mass–position pairing — the multiplicative Euler-product structure — is load-bearing everywhere: the unperturbed object of any analysis must be the full von Mangoldt comb.

**No one-prime induction.** Inserting the atoms one at a time into a fixed window (exact per-atom lag rows), the pure-archimedean start is catastrophically subcritical ($\tau(0)\sim-10^{14}$ or $G_+$ indefinite) and the *last* sign crossing sits at $u^*/U = 0.991$–$0.999$ in **every** insertion order; edge-first ordering reaches stable positivity only at 100% of the atoms. Positivity is a property of the complete comb: induction must run in a flow/deformation coordinate, not in comb content.

**The flow direction.** The ladder deformation of the IIKS generators closes in a degree-2 Lax dictionary $\{f,g,Yf,Yg,Y^2f,Y^2g\}$ (residual medians $0.244/0.248$; degree 1 fails) — isomonodromy-compatible — but the raw coefficient basis is ill-conditioned noise (the port nodes cluster near $y=1$); the integrated tau-transfer identity $\Delta\log|\tau| = \log|\det(I + (I-D_k)^{-1}\Delta D)|$ is exact ($6\cdot10^{-11}$). Finding the conditioned flow coordinate is the named open object.
