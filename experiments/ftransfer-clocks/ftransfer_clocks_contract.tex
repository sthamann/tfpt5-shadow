\section*{1. Where the contract starts}

The $F_{\mathrm{transfer}}$ interface (\texttt{CONTRACT.F.01}) has its PGL$_2$ \emph{syntax} closed:
one common parabolic action on the discrete half, and on the native continuous half the classified
degeneration $g_{\mathrm{Boltzmann}}=g_{\mathrm{pole}}$ (hyperbolic, forced identical by the single-flow
theorem \texttt{v425}), $g_{\mathrm{QCD}}$ parabolic and exactly conjugate to the base translation,
$g_{\mathrm{relic}}$ the identity (\texttt{v632\_ftransfer\_pgl2.py}). At jet level the native wall-time
Schwarzian quadruple is
\begin{equation}
\Bigl\{\; -\tfrac{\Delta^2}{2},\; -\tfrac{\Delta^2}{2},\; 0,\; \text{degenerate} \;\Bigr\},
\qquad \Delta = 6\ln\tfrac32 ,
\label{eq:quadruple}
\end{equation}
each flow M\"obius \emph{in its own clock} (\texttt{v578\_native\_jets.py}): the obstruction to a shared
projective dynamics is the \emph{clock}, not the projective structure. Both internal-clock rescues are
machine-killed: strong thermal time (a modular generator is diagonalizable; the QCD clock map is parabolic
with no real-diagonalizable logarithm, \texttt{v723}), and conditioned modular flows of one internal mother
state (loxodromic, wrong classes, \texttt{v724}). \emph{Diagnosis:} the clocks must be external --- and
without clocks fixed \emph{in advance}, every jet statement is arbitrarily reparametrizable. This contract
fixes the clocks.

\section*{3. Transport and the preregistered prediction}

The common projective connection lives on the log-scale line $\sigma=\ln(\mu/M_Z)$. The four dictionaries
are affine in their log charts with slopes $(-1,-1,+1,-1)$ \emph{frozen}; every dimensionful anchor
($m_{\mathrm{ref}}$, $M_1$, $\mu_0$, $a_0$, the thermal matching constant $\xi=1$) enters as an affine
offset only and drops out of every Schwarzian and every conjugacy class --- the contract tests exactly the
anchor-\emph{free} jet content, consistent with the \texttt{v723} anchor census
$\{\Lambda_{\mathrm{QCD}}\ \text{unit},\ \theta_i,\ C_p\}$ staying external. Each channel exports the
order-3 jet $(y,y',y'',y''')$ of its native trajectory in its own frozen clock at the frozen base point;
transport is the Schwarzian cocycle
\begin{equation}
\sch{y\circ h}{\sigma} \;=\; \bigl(\sch{y}{x}\circ h\bigr)\,(h')^{2} \;+\; \sch{h}{\sigma},
\qquad
\sch{e^{a\sigma}}{\sigma} = -\tfrac{a^2}{2},
\end{equation}
with $h$ the frozen dictionary. All kernel steps are symbolic (sympy exact); data enter only through base
points and dictionary-validity checks.

\textbf{Prediction FTC.P1 (frozen).} There exists \emph{one} constant-coefficient Riccati equation
$y'=c_0+c_1y+c_2y^2$ on $\sigma$ satisfied by all non-degenerate transported jets. Since all non-constant
solutions of one such equation share the constant Schwarzian $S=-(c_1^2-4c_0c_2)/2$, this is equivalent to:
the transported Schwarzians of $\Fpole$, $\FB$, $\FQ$ are \emph{equal} --- the native quadruple
\eqref{eq:quadruple} collapses into \emph{one} PGL$_2$ class under the fixed physical clocks. The seam
channels transport to $\sch{q}{\sigma}=\sch{y_B}{\sigma}=-\Delta^2/2$ exactly, so the prediction forces
\begin{equation}
\sch{\alpha_s}{\sigma} \;=\; -\frac{\Delta^2}{2} \;=\; -18\ln^2\tfrac32 \;=\; -2.9592351700\ldots
\end{equation}
If it does not hold, the contract dies. The degenerate $\FR$ carries no class information (its content is
the anchor $\theta_i$); it is scored on the kill criteria and dictionary validity only --- fixed-point
membership of a constant is \emph{not} scored, because with a per-channel M\"obius frame it would be
vacuous.

\section*{4. Kill criteria (frozen)}

\begin{itemize}\itemsep2pt
\item[\textbf{K1}] \textsc{no-common-connection}: with the clocks and dictionaries exactly as frozen, the
transported Schwarzians differ (symbolic inequality, no tolerance). On kill, the executor must exhibit the
reparametrization that \emph{would} be needed per failing pair, documenting the forbidden freedom.
\item[\textbf{K2}] \textsc{free-reparam-needed}: a pass is achievable only by a post-freeze, per-channel
change of gauge or dictionary (re-sloped or non-affine dictionary, either e-fold gauge, $\xi$, $\mu_0$,
scheme, window, frame re-fit). Machine form: the executor runs against the byte-frozen YAML (sha256
recorded); any deviation needed for a pass is K2.
\item[\textbf{K3}] \textsc{second-clock-needed}: any channel needs a second time variable to express its
exported jet (e.g.\ $\FQ$ needing both $\mu$ and $T$). Each export must be a function of exactly one clock,
asserted structurally on the symbolic exports.
\end{itemize}
